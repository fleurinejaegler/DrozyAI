WHAT’S BEHIND THE READING

Signals first. A combined model next.

The live indicator uses observed eye closure. Heart rate is displayed alongside it and does not yet change the level. The trained TCN and watch model are available through the local API for matching features. HRV, wrist motion and blood oxygen remain unavailable until their live data paths are verified.

View prototype rules and limitations +

Eye closure is estimated from face-model expression scores, using a threshold of 0.55. This is an eye-closure proxy, not a validated PERCLOS measurement. A completed closure of 80–800 ms counts as a blink. The rolling fraction uses up to 60 seconds of valid observed time and needs at least 15 seconds and 80% tracking coverage.

Elevated: current closure ≥0.8 s or rolling fraction ≥12%. High: current closure ≥1.5 s or rolling fraction ≥25%. These are adjustable demonstration rules, not thresholds learned or validated on your dataset. Tracking gaps are excluded; missing signals never imply low drowsiness.

Heart rate comes from Bluetooth Heart Rate Measurement notifications. No beat intervals, raw optical signal, motion or SpO₂ are inferred from BPM. The trend comparison uses the first available 30-second portion of the displayed history; it is descriptive and does not establish drowsiness.

Face landmarks: Google MediaPipe. Bluetooth bridge: Bleak. Camera frames remain in browser memory; no video recording or upload is implemented. Feature files selected in the model test are sent only to this Mac's local server, used in memory and not saved.
